# Frentes renovados — Paso Viento Helado

Generados con image_gen integrada. Tres PNG RGBA 1536 × 1024, transparencia real, variaciones para los tres fondos. Se conservan los originales en `anteriores/`.

El plano `FondoFrente` ya existía. Las tres entradas `fondosPasoVientoHelado` tenían `fondoFrente` vacío: se reconectan con `frente1.mat`, `frente2.mat` y `frente3.mat`. Se mantienen geometría, cámara y referencias de texturas. Escala UV vertical 1, offset vertical 0, para que los bordes asomen por encima de la barra inferior sin muestrear fuera de la textura. Clamp evita repeticiones al salir del encuadre. Materiales transparentes en cola 3999 (máxima admitida por Standard/Fade, después de las unidades), sin brillo ni metalicidad artificial.

## Frente 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a VERY SUBTLE foreground depth overlay for an existing 2D/2.5D tactical battle, ONE transparent RGBA PNG 1536 x 1024 landscape.
The reference image is ONLY the painting style, materials and palette reference for this foreground. Do NOT reproduce its scenery, sky, floor, mountains or architecture.
Create just TWO SMALL, LOW, ASYMMETRICAL snowy foreground fragments entering from the extreme BOTTOM-LEFT and BOTTOM-RIGHT corners. This overlay is drawn IN FRONT of all battle units to give a hint of depth. It must barely peek into the frame, never cover playable tiles or characters.
STRICT SCREEN-SPACE FOOTPRINT: at least 95% of the entire canvas is genuinely transparent. All visible art is restricted to the bottom 15% of the canvas AND the outer left 13% / outer right 13% of the width. Nothing in the central 74% of the canvas, even along the bottom edge. Left fragment may extend up at most 130px from the bottom and inward at most 175px from the left; right fragment up at most 100px and inward at most 180px from the right. Avoid big peaks or tall vertical objects. Both pieces continue out of the bottom/side edges naturally, so they look like near scenery just outside view.
ILLUSTRATION: match the reference's sober illustrated dark fantasy, broad deliberate rock/shadow planes, restrained natural volume, low visual noise, crisp meaningful edges, small softly painted snow cap. Foreground a little darker than background, but no solid black silhouettes or outlines. Very few distinct forms, no pebble carpet. No full frame, ground plane, drop shadow around the cutout, atmospheric gradient, screen fog, background glow or opaque rectangle.
Alpha requirements: actual transparent PNG, clean natural object-edge alpha only; everything else completely transparent including ALL sky, central field and top 85%. Do not paint white, black, checkerboards or any backdrop. No text, labels, UI, grid, characters, mountains, buildings or watermark.
VARIANT 1, the reddish/umber and muted mauve winter pass: left corner has one low broad iron-rich reddish-brown slate ledge with a thin dull ivory snow lip, the darker exposed rock turning subtly plum in shadow. At the extreme right, a smaller low angular snow-dusted rock with a SHORT weathered broken twig barely rising beside it, tucked at the outer edge. Tiny shapes only, not a tall stump or a bush. Keep the same subdued mineral palette and painted finish as the reference image. Return only this sparse transparent overlay, not the complete environment.
