Herramienta: image_gen integrada. La referencia exacta adjunta por el usuario se conserva en `referencia-usuario.png`. El prompt largo anterior y sus cambios de composición, variantes y capas quedan descartados para esta tarea.

## Fortaleza

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: actual production background texture for an existing dark medieval fantasy tactical RPG. One opaque RGB PNG, 1536 x 1024, landscape 3:2.
INPUT ROLES: Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: preserve its scene identity and camera layout. Image 2 is the user's authoritative APPROVED ART STYLE reference: match its drawing language, degree of illustration, restrained naturalism, painterly volume, strong solid shadows, cold slate palette, sparse detail, broad snow shapes and quiet floor. Do not copy its mountain arrangement or tribal camp into this different scene.
Primary request: Redraw image 1 into exactly the same visual family as image 2. The old background is overly granular and packed with repeated rocks and pointed peaks. Replace that noise with intentional larger clean forms, distinct asymmetrical silhouettes, and selective meaningful detail. Retain convincing natural rock volume and snow thickness; no caricature or thick cartoon outlines, no photographic rendering.
STYLE LOCK: same substantial charcoal/slate shadow masses, muted steel-blue light, gray-white snow, softly modeled cloud masses and atmospheric ridge separation as image 2. Use controlled smooth painted transitions and selective crisp structural edges. Avoid tiny choppy brush flecks, canvas grain, stippling, all-over crosshatching, low-poly facets or a sharpness filter. Strong shadow design on side cliffs with calmer center values. Distant formations are fewer, broader and distinct; no forest of repeated triangular needles. Rock detail belongs in meaningful breaks and planes, not hundreds of pebbles.
FLOOR: same visual treatment as the approved reference: broad open dark slate with restrained natural volume, a FEW subtle irregular cracks, sparse larger wind-shaped snow tongues mainly near side edges. Remove the carpet of small rocks and white streaks. Keep existing perspective and open ground footprint for the separately rendered tactical board, with softer detail behind units. DO NOT adopt a new camera layout, raise the horizon or turn the lower two thirds into a featureless uniform floor. Preserve the source scene's vanishing point, general horizon level, major side-cliff footprint and overall proportions.
CAMERA SAFETY: fully paint all four edges to maintain the original coverage during battle camera zoom/tilt/yaw. No frame, vignette, crop or transparent voids. No changes to image dimensions.
Environment only: no people/creatures, UI, grid, painted board, text, logo or watermark. No large painted snow particles; engine supplies weather.
SCENE 1 — fortress pass. Preserve the winding snowy pass between steep cliffs, the distant gothic fortress centered slightly to the right, and a small number of sparse dead conifers/broken trunks confined to flanks. Keep the fortress location, scale and distinctive dominant silhouette, but clarify its architecture into a few coherent tower/building masses rather than dozens of identical needle-like turrets. Keep the original recession and open foreground. Simplify surrounding mountain chains into a few distinct overlapping ridges while preserving the pass's enclosing shape. Make this clearly the fortress scene, not the tribal valley from the style reference. Deliver exactly one finished repaint.
