# Bosque Ardiente — estilo aprobado

Generación con image_gen integrada. Tres fondos RGB y tres frentes RGBA, todos PNG 1536 × 1024; originales conservados en `anteriores/`.

Criterios tomados de lo aprobado para Paso Viento Helado: ilustración sobria con volumen natural, sombras definidas, superficies limpias sin granulado, menos repeticiones y paletas diferenciadas. Se conservan perspectiva, disposición y cobertura de cámara originales; no se aplica el prompt largo descartado. Frentes casi totalmente transparentes, dos fragmentos pequeños en las esquinas inferiores, sin invadir el centro.

Integración: se reemplazan las seis texturas en sus rutas originales conservando GUID. Los materiales de frente 2 y 3 apuntaban a la imagen del frente 1: se corrigen para usar sus propias variaciones. Igual que en Paso, los frentes usan cola transparente 3999, metalicidad/brillo cero, escala UV vertical 1 y Clamp. Los vínculos fondo/frente de la escena ya eran correctos y se conservan. No se modifica lógica de tutorial, combate, cámara ni ambiente.

## Fondo 1

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: actual production background for a 2D/2.5D dark medieval fantasy tactical RPG, ONE opaque RGB PNG 1536 x 1024, landscape 3:2.
INPUT ROLES: Image 1 is the FOREST EDIT TARGET; preserve its original scene identity, perspective, composition and camera coverage. Image 2 is the already approved Paso Viento Helado painting used ONLY as an authoritative ART STYLE reference: sober illustrated digital painting, believable but controlled natural volume, strong deliberate shadow masses, clean broad forms, selective material detail and atmospheric depth. Do NOT copy snow, mountains, camps or any subject from that style reference into this burnt forest.
Primary request: Completely redraw image 1 in that same coherent illustrative art style. Its current all-over granular rendering, repetitive fine twigs and pebbles should be replaced with larger purposeful shapes, distinct asymmetrical tree silhouettes and readable light/shadow design. Remain semi-realistic, not cartoonish, flat cel-shaded, low-poly or photorealistic.
Forest art: natural flowing trunk/root forms, a small number of distinct gnarled charred trees on the flanks, uneven branches with intentional shapes and varied spacing, groups of simpler distant tree silhouettes separated by soft smoke. Preserve the dark burned-forest identity, but REMOVE the wall of hundreds of identical branch forks, repeated vertical trunks and tiny rocky debris. No dense black mesh of twigs. Selective bark damage rather than repeated stripe texture.
Ground: retain the same broad open tactical clearing and source ground perspective/vanishing point/horizon. Repaint it as quiet dark ash-covered earth with broad subtle mineral/soil variations, a few faint scuffs or sparse hairline cracks; not a carpet of stones, sticks or high-contrast leaf flecks. No new roots, logs, bushes, fire, rocks or objects obstructing the central playable footprint. Do not raise the horizon, redesign the camera layout, or make the entire lower two thirds a uniform blank fill. Keep the terrain naturally painted with low contrast behind units.
Fire/smoke: preserve understated smoldering roots and a few restrained ember glows on the SIDES, not a giant wall of flame. Clean atmospheric smoke volumes, small meaningful warm accents, no painted particle shower or screen effects (the game animates those).
Composition invariants: same 1536x1024 canvas, original main side-trunk/ledge footprint and existing landmarks in their original places. Keep center and foreground open; all margins naturally complete for camera zoom, tilt and yaw. No black vignette/frame or shallow-focus blur.
Palette must belong to this forest, with a distinct material/atmosphere mix per scene as specified below; do not blindly reuse the snowy reference's blue-gray colors. Stronger contrast in side trees, softer values at center; dark units must remain readable against the floor.
Avoid: all-over grain/stippling/canvas noise, repeated tiny stones, repetitive tree/branch patterns, over-sharpened bark, cartoon outlines, photography, glossy 3D, UI, text, logos, characters, monsters, tactical grid, board, snow or mountains. Exactly one finished environment image.
SCENE 1 — copper embers and cold ash. Keep the huge charred trunk at far left, the crooked broken trunk/fallen wood behind the left side of the clearing, the stepped dark rock ledge on the right and the original very distant ruined silhouette on the upper-right ridge, now simplified and faint behind smoke. It must remain a tiny atmospheric landmark, not become a new dominant castle. Color identity: muted copper/rust in exposed scorched bark and ember-lit root planes, cool charcoal and pewter-blue smoke, ashen umber-gray floor. A few low amber embers below side roots. The light opening behind the trees is diffuse cold gray. Match the approved painting's measured level of illustration and natural material volume.

Create ONLY two very small low asymmetrical bits of nearby burnt forest scenery peeking in at the BOTTOM-LEFT and BOTTOM-RIGHT corners. They are drawn in front of the battle field, so they must remain subtle and never intrude on playable units.
